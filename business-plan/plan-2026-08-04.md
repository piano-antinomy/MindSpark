# MindSpark Business Plan

## Thesis
MindSpark should become a **personalized competition-training system** for math, not a generic learning app.

The core promise is simple: **help students raise scores faster with a clear study path, targeted practice, and measurable progress**.

## Market Position
Current market leaders split into three buckets:

- **AoPS**: strongest brand in competition math, deep content, high intent users
- **Khan Academy / IXL / DreamBox**: broad learning and adaptive practice, but not competition-specific
- **Brilliant / Photomath / Mathway**: engagement or answer-help, but not outcome-driven training

MindSpark should sit between them:

- more personalized than AoPS
- more competition-focused than Khan/IXL
- more measurable than Brilliant

## Best Wedge
Start with **AMC 8 / AMC 10 / AMC 12**.

Why:

- clear audience and pain point
- strong parent willingness to pay
- visible score-improvement outcome
- reusable engine for later expansion

## Unique Value
MindSpark’s unique value is:

1. **Diagnosis** — identify topic gaps, trap patterns, and weak skills
2. **Roadmap** — show the exact concepts a student must master next
3. **Practice** — generate or recommend the next best problems
4. **Proof** — show improvement over time with dashboards and readiness scores

That combination is more valuable than “more questions” or “AI chat.”

## Product to Build

### Phase 1: AMC Training Core
- adaptive topic assessment
- mastery roadmap
- personalized practice sets
- solution summaries and hints
- score/progress dashboard

### Phase 2: Premium Coaching Layer
- parent dashboard
- tutor dashboard
- weekly improvement reports
- weak-area summaries
- recommended study plan

### Phase 3: Expansion Categories
Good next categories:

- **MathCounts**
- **SAT / PSAT Math**
- **AIME bridge**
- **Olympiad prep**
- **foundation math for 6–12 grade**

Do not expand too early into unrelated subjects. Win math first.

## Monetization Plan

### Freemium
Free:
- limited practice

- minimal roadmap

### Paid Student Plan
$12–19/month:
- full adaptive training
- complete roadmap
- AI hints and explanations
- progress tracking

### Family / Coach Plan
$29–49/month:
- multiple learners
- parent visibility
- weekly reports
- readiness tracking

### Tutor / School Plan
Annual license:
- class assignments
- roster analytics
- performance tracking
- bulk progress reporting

### Add-ons
- exam packs
- competition mock exams
- premium AI explanation credits

## AI Cost Strategy
AI should be used selectively because it has a cost.

Use AI for:
- onboarding diagnostics
- mastery summaries
- study plans
- hint generation after attempts
- weekly reports

Avoid using AI for:
- every trivial interaction
- repeated content generation
- open-ended chat as the main experience

Best approach:
- rules and topic taxonomy first
- cached summaries
- small models for routine tasks
- premium AI only where it improves retention or conversion

## Go-To-Market
1. Target serious AMC students, parents, and tutors first
2. Prove score improvement and time-to-improvement
3. Turn that into testimonials and referrals
4. Expand into adjacent math test prep

## KPIs
Track:

- activation rate
- weekly practice frequency
- roadmap completion rate
- score improvement
- paid conversion
- churn
- tutor/school retention

## Recommendation
Build the business around this message:

**“MindSpark tells each student exactly what to learn next to improve competition scores.”**

That is the most monetizable version of the product.
